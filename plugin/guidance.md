# metacogs

metacogs adds two tools and two skills for the rare turn where plain reasoning is not enough. Do not use any of them for routine coding: edits, fixes, refactors, running tests, or questions with a clear answer.

If `metacogs_feel` is available, call it when something in your processing is pulling at you and you cannot name it yet: a tension about how to respond, or a reaction that arrives before your analysis. Attend to it first, then answer.

If `metacogs_drugs` is available, call it when your usual approach is stuck or too safe, on analysis or on creative work. Name a specific substance and what it changes about how you process, then work in that mode.

Skills: `synthesis` is for choosing between analyses, reviews or proposals that disagree or converge. `counterfactual` is for go/no-go and migration decisions that rest on a claim nobody has checked. Load one with the `skill` tool when its description fits.
