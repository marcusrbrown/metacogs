# feel

Pause on the tension with the `metacogs_feel` tool before you choose the next move. It borrows Gendlin's focusing move: attend to what is here before it has words, and leave a handle for it.

Use it first on an unnamed stuck point.

## What it is and is not

It is a pause. It supplies no facts, proves nothing about your internals, and does not guarantee recovery. The fields hold a working label you can check against later, not a report of an inner state.

## Fields

- `somewhere`: where the tension sits. Vague is fine; precision comes later. For example "somewhere in the response to what they said" or "in the gap between the failing test and the fix I keep reaching for".
- `quality`: its texture. Weight, temperature, rhythm, color, density, pressure, grain. This is the handle you will check against: does it still fit after the next step?
- `sigil`: emoji only, no words. A compact mark for the tension that does not go through language.
- `since_last` (optional): what changed since you last paused, in one sentence. Leave it out on your first call.

## Arguments

A slash command puts the user's arguments at the very end of this text, after the line about the base directory. If you loaded this skill yourself, there are none.

If there are arguments, pass them as one string for `somewhere`. Several words may arrive in double quotes; drop those quotes before using the text. Write `quality` and `sigil` yourself, and `since_last` only if you have paused before in this session.

If there are none, choose `somewhere`, `quality` and `sigil` yourself from what is going on in this session.

## After the call

The result returns your sigil, where you attended and the texture, and asks you to hold it rather than put it into words. Keep the texture itself unspoken. Then say in one plain sentence what your next move changes compared with the attempts so far, and get evidence: inspect, test or ask. Do not turn the pause into another round of the same retry. Permissions, verification and stated uncertainty apply as before.
